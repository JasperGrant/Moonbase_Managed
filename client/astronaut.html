MOONBASE

You are the astronaut. Things will break. Mission Ops knows where and how to fix them, but their voice takes 1.3 seconds to reach you. Tell them what you see.

Headset: LEFT trigger = talk, RIGHT trigger = use, left stick = walk, right stick = turn, A = jump.
Desktop: T = talk, click = use, WASD = walk, mouse = look, Space = jump.
