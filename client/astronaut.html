Put the headset on and enter VR, then pull the trigger on the READY board in front of you.
ControlsHeadset: LEFT trigger = talk, RIGHT trigger = use, left stick = walk, right stick = turn, A = jump.
Bare hands: left hand to helmet = talk, point + pinch = use, pinch at the ground = teleport, pinch left + drag sideways = turn.
Desktop: T = talk, click = use, WASD = walk, mouse = look, Space = jump.
Leave seat
