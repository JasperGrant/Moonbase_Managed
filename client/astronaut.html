Put the headset on and enter VR. A card for each seat stands in front of you: point at yours and pull the trigger when you are set.
ControlsNothing is drawn on your visor: your gauges are on your left wrist, and reading them is what turns the radio on.
Headset: raise your LEFT wrist like a watch, where you can read it = radio on (the microphone on it lights up), hold TRIGGER (or GRIP) on a tool = take it in that hand, let go = drop it (let go mid-swing to throw it), LEFT trigger with nothing in reach = radio without looking, RIGHT trigger with nothing in reach = point at something close (the hatch, your card in the waiting room), left stick = walk, right stick = turn, A = jump. Crouch by crouching.
Bare hands: pinch on a thing = grab it, left wrist raised like a watch = radio on, left hand to helmet = radio without looking, point + pinch = use, pinch at the ground = teleport, pinch left + drag sideways = turn, pinch both empty hands and pull apart = zoom.
Desktop: click = take a tool or use it (hold to keep brushing or welding), Q = toss the tool in your hand, hold Tab = radio + wrist gauges, WASD = walk, mouse = look, C = crouch, F = patch suit, G = generator, Space = jump.
Leave seat
