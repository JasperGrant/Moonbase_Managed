Mission

The referee's console. It is not a seat: nothing here counts toward the crew, and nothing is delayed. Start now skips the ready-up and starts (or restarts) the mission with whoever is in. Reset ends the game and sends everyone back to the landing page, where 2 or 4 players is chosen again.

Seats

Kick clears a seat by dropping every connection that holds it, so a seat that reads taken with nobody at it can be taken again at once. The person kicked sees a notice and can rejoin.
